3. Methodology

Methodology

Data and sample

Resumes and human-written job descriptions are taken from public datasets. Records are labeled frontend, backend, UX, or infrastructure engineer from role phrases in the resume or the job title. The controlled sample uses 2 resumes and 2 jobs in each role. The broader sample uses 3 resumes and 5 jobs in each role.

NLP and skill extraction

Text is lower-cased and reduced to word tokens, with English stop words removed. Skill terms are phrases that occur more often inside one IT role than outside it. Resumes that are scored are excluded from this extraction. Skill coverage is the number of skill terms found on both the resume and the job, divided by the number of skill terms on the job.

TF-IDF match score

The match score is TF-IDF cosine on the job description and the job title, combined with skill coverage. Jobs are ranked by this score.

score = 0.80 × cosine(TF-IDF resume, job description)
+ 0.10 × cosine(TF-IDF resume, job title)
+ 0.10 × skill coverage

Semantic matching

Semantic matching is the cosine similarity between sentence embeddings of the resume and of the job title plus description. The model is all-MiniLM-L6-v2. Long documents are split into sections, embedded, and averaged, so the score is not limited to the opening lines. This score is compared with the match score. It is not added into the formula above.

Evaluation

A job is correct when its IT role equals the resume’s IT role. Best match means rank 1 is correct. Top-3 means a correct role appears among the first three. Mean reciprocal rank is 1 divided by the rank of the first correct role, averaged over resumes.

7. Conclusion

Conclusion

On the controlled sample the match score reached 50.0% best match (MRR 0.671). On the broader sample it reached 25.0% best match (MRR 0.508). Jobs were ranked by the match score. On the controlled sample, semantic best match was lower than the match score (37.5% versus 50.0%). On the broader sample, semantic best match was higher than the match score (33.3% versus 25.0%).

Limitations

  • Each role contributes only 2 or 3 resumes, so the percentages illustrate the method rather than estimate a population rate.
  • Role labels are assigned from phrases in the resume and the job title.

Remaining proposed work

  • The interactive application for recruiters and job seekers is not built.
