Method

How a resume is scored

match score = 80% body TF-IDF + 10% title TF-IDF + 10% skill coverage

80% body, 10% title, 10% skill. Body and title are TF-IDF cosines. Skill coverage is the share of the job’s skill terms found on the resume. This score orders the jobs.

  • Data. Public resumes and human-written jobs, limited to Frontend, Backend, UX, and Infrastructure engineer.
  • Semantic score. Cosine of sentence embeddings. Comparison only; it is not added into the match score.
  • Evaluation. Best match, Top-3, and MRR. Same IT role counts as correct.
